Replica Exchange

Each replica samples \(V(x)\) using Metropolis-Hastings: at every step a new position \(x' = x + \mathcal{U}(-s,\, s)\) is proposed, where \(s = 0.55\sqrt{T}\), and accepted with probability \(\min\!\left(1,\, e^{-\Delta V/T}\right)\), where \(\Delta V = V(x') - V(x)\).

Periodically, neighboring replicas attempt to swap temperatures, in itself a Metropolis step, accepted with probability \(\min\!\left(1,\, e^{\,\delta}\right)\) where \(\delta = \left(\tfrac{1}{T_i} - \tfrac{1}{T_j}\right)(V_i - V_j)\), so detailed balance is preserved at every level.
